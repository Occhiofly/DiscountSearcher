Video game deals, four stores in a single app.

Discount Searcher shows the most recent deals from Steam, Epic Games Store, GOG and Humble Store and filters them by genre, minimum discount and title. Pick the store from the menu, without opening their sites one by one.

The product

From the search to the deal, in four steps.

The application is built around a single screen: you search, filter, compare and open the deal in the store. Select a step to see it.

Choose a store and start the search: the list shows its most recent deals, with price and discount percentage.

How it works

From the download to the first search.

  1. 01

    Download

    Download the ZIP archive, extract the folder and start DiscountSearcher.exe. Python is not needed.

  2. 02

    Sign up

    Choose a username and a password (at least 8 characters) and give a Gmail address and your date of birth.

  3. 03

    Verify

    Type the 6-digit code you receive by email, valid for 15 minutes. The account is active.

  4. 04

    Search

    Choose the store, set genre, minimum discount or part of the title and press Search.

  5. 05

    Find again

    The games you open go into the history, and you find them again even when signing in from another computer.

Supported stores

Four stores, one single program.

Discount Searcher gets the information about the deals through the public CheapShark API, a service that collects the discounts of several digital stores. Game genres come from the public Steam API.

Independent project. Discount Searcher is not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by any of the listed stores or by CheapShark. The prices shown are the ones provided by those sources: the valid price is always the one shown by the store at the moment of purchase.

    Account and security

    An account that follows you, protected by concrete measures.

    The account makes your history available on any computer. Here is how it is protected, without inflated promises.

    Questions about security

    • Sign-up and email verification

      When you sign up you receive a 6-digit code, valid for 15 minutes. Until the email is confirmed, the account cannot sign in.

    • Passwords never stored in clear text

      Only a value derived from the password stays on the server, computed with a random element that is different for every user.

    • Protected sign-in

      When something is wrong, the message does not reveal whether it is the username or the password. Every sign-in creates a session the server can revoke.

    • Repeated attempts blocked

      After several failed attempts in a row on the same username, signing in is suspended temporarily.

    • Password recovery

      A 6-digit code sent to the registered email address lets you set a new password. After the reset, every connected computer is signed out.

    • Changes confirmed and notified

      Username and password can only be changed by entering the current password; the email also needs two codes, one sent to the old address and one to the new one. Every change is notified by email to the previous address.

    • Several computers, one account

      Sign in from different computers with the same credentials and find your history again. When you change the password, the other computers are signed out.
