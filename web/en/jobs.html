        Before you apply

        What is fair to know.

        You need the app account

        The application is linked to your account, like tickets: it is the only way to bring the answers back to you safely. If you do not have one, download the application and create it: it takes a Gmail address and a few minutes.

        It stays between you and the team

        What you write is seen only by you and the team, from the ticket page. Do not send documents, payment details or passwords: applying does not require them.

        Times and arrangements

        There are few of us and there is no guaranteed response time. How much time you can give, how to organise and any other arrangement is discussed directly in the ticket, before any commitment.

        One application at a time

        If you change your mind about the role or want to add something, reply in the ticket you already opened instead of opening another one: at most five tickets can be opened in one day.

        Tell us what you can do.
